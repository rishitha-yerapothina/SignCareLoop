Simple by design

From a sign to a message.

The recognition workflow turns an available sign gesture into a predicted label. The application can then present text and, where supported, speak that text aloud.

01

Capture a sign

The webcam captures the gesture. The recognition pipeline processes the movement and extracts features for the model.

02

Predict and display

The trained model predicts a supported sign class and displays the prediction with its confidence score.

03

Communicate

The recognized text can be spoken aloud. Hospital quick messages also provide a separate way to communicate common needs.

About the project

Communication should not be a barrier to care.

SignCareLoop is an educational prototype exploring how computer vision and machine learning can support communication between Indian Sign Language users and hearing hospital staff. It is intended for demonstration and further development, not as a replacement for qualified interpreters or clinical judgment.

Explore the app → Open live recognition

Prototype notice

Recognition is limited to the classes supported by the installed model and may be inaccurate. Confidence scores are not guarantees of correctness. In real healthcare situations, confirm important messages with the patient and use a qualified interpreter or established communication support when needed.
